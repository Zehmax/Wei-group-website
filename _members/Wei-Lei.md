I received the B.S. degree in 2009 in the School of Material Science and Engineering and the M.S. degree in 2013 in the School of Energy and Power Engineering at Xi’an Jiaotong University, China. In 2014, I, as a Ph.D. student, joined Prof. T.S. ZHAO’s group in Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and received the Ph.D. degree in 2017.
My research is mainly focused on energy storage systems such as the design of flow batteries, the application of nanomaterials in energy storage systems, and heat and mass transfer issues in batteries. In the past years, although my major changed from material science&engineering to power engineering, and then to mechanical engineering, I have always been studying the theory and application of energy storage systems.
